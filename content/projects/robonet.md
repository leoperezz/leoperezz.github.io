[Robonet](https://robonet.nonhuman.site) is my robotics startup—**The internet of Robotics**. Our goal is to scale full-body datasets so that robots can learn whole-body policies, not just isolated arm or gripper motions.

By massively expanding the diversity and realism of full-body data, Robonet aims to make it possible for robots to coordinate balance, contact, and manipulation in a unified way. This is key if we want robots that can move through the world as naturally as humans do, and not just perform a few pre-programmed tasks in constrained environments.

In the long term, I see Robonet as part of a broader ecosystem where embodied intelligence, large-scale data, and open infrastructure converge—so that robotics can benefit from the same kind of scaling that transformed language and vision models.
