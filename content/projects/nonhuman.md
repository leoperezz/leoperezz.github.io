[NONHUMAN](https://www.nonhuman.site) is Peru's first Embodied AI lab. This post is a personal account of why NONHUMAN was founded and what its ultimate goal is.

![NONHUMAN LATAM](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtpiuha91/image/upload/v1772373358/image_qnhfnq.png)

It's no surprise that building DeepTech in LATAM is difficult—especially in a field that is still new or only recently gaining momentum worldwide. Nevertheless, I believe we need to start cultivating a **bootstrapping culture** within our ecosystem: someone always has to lay the first stone. I'm not just talking about building your career or learning this field on your own; I'm talking about **building a community**—pushing the cart forward and showing that this is possible, that we can do it with whatever resources we have, and that we must do it.

That said, I'd like to share some observations from within the community, especially in Peru. Too often things are driven by *how they look* rather than *what they actually mean*. This phenomenon may not be local, but I feel the tendency is stronger here. I've seen and met many companies that claim to be something they're not, promoting technology that doesn't compete or isn't even close to the cutting edge. I've seen universities that aren't really prepared for this shift yet still present themselves as leading change in education and adapting to this new era of Artificial Intelligence. It's more noise than substance. That's why I believe NONHUMAN matters: we want to bring a culture of **builders**—of doing rather than just talking.

---

## Long-term mission

NONHUMAN's long-term mission is to become a lab that serves as a **seedbed for future generations**. Right now we're operating as a startup, working on a problem that I feel is underexplored—but in the end this will go by another name. NONHUMAN must be a lab in LATAM that advances Embodied AI from a position that is level with the rest of the world: a place where people *build* rather than just follow trends or make technology that amounts to calling APIs. Beyond that, we want to be a community where we can shift the mindset toward building things—especially in this era when coding has been largely automated. In deeptech, you have a place here where you can actually do it.

---

## Why I believe we're on the right track

I've met many people at NONHUMAN who are exceptional—genuinely very strong. As we often say in LATAM: **there is talent, but support is lacking.** These people tell me that we operate differently: we actually do great things, we experiment, we read, we discuss truly interesting topics. One of my heroes from when I was starting university—[Arturo Deza](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arturo-deza-9b12324a/) (PostDoc MIT & Harvard)—considers us, together with [Artificio](https://artificio.org/), one of the two frontier groups in Peru. Coming from him, that fills me with pride.

I truly hope this keeps growing and can help the next generations of engineers and builders who come after me—so they have a place where they can do **real deeptech**.
